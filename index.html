Tomhen Services: homepage shortlist

These are 8 designs for a subcontracting, contract staffing, surveying and outsourced project services business that supports infrastructure clients. Every design uses the same wording, so only the look changes. Tap a card to open the full page, and use the pill at the bottom left to come back. Try them on your phone too.

The company name, contact details, stats, projects and accreditations are placeholders. Client names and logos are left as blanks on purpose: none of these pages claims a relationship with any real organisation.
